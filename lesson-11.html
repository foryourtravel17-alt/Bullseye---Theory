Lesson Summary & Key Takeaways

This capstone connects the course into a repeatable study workflow: identify the asset and timeframe; describe the broader market condition; mark meaningful swing structure and zones; observe price action without treating patterns as certainty; consider breakout or retest scenarios; define invalidation and quantify risk; then record the decision and review execution. The goal is a consistent process, not a prediction machine. Distinguish chart facts from assumptions, keep risk within a preplanned limit, and review both successful and unsuccessful examples. No framework removes market risk or guarantees profits.

Apply it: Write down the observation, your interpretation, the uncertainty, and what evidence would change your view. Educational content only; not personalized financial advice.

REVISION FRAMEWORK

Before any hypothetical chart decision

01 · CONTEXT

What market am I studying?

Identify the asset, timeframe and broader context before focusing on a single candle or level.

02 · STRUCTURE

What is price doing?

Study highs, lows, trend, range and important zones. Describe what is visible before predicting.

03 · EVIDENCE

What changed?

Look for reactions, breakouts, retests or other evidence that supports or contradicts the study idea.

04 · RISK

What can be lost?

Define risk before position size. A possible reward does not remove the possibility of loss.

05 · EXECUTION

What are the rules?

Use a written process rather than changing the plan because of excitement, fear or impatience.

06 · REVIEW

What did I learn?

Journal the setup, decision, result and lesson. Review the process instead of judging yourself from one outcome.
