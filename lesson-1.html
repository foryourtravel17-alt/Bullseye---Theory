What Is Trading?

01 • THE BASICS

What does “trading” mean?

Trading means buying something and selling it later, hoping to sell it for more than you paid. The difference between the buying price and selling price can produce a profit or a loss.

BUYChoose an asset at one price.
→
PRICE CHANGESThe market moves.
→
SELLThe difference becomes profit or loss.

Trading

Buying and selling with the aim of making a profit while accepting the possibility of loss.

Trading vs. Investing

This lesson frames trading as buying and selling more quickly, while investing generally involves buying and holding for years.

Core reality

A trade cannot guarantee a profit. The price can move in either direction after you buy.

02 • THE MARKET

Buyers, sellers and price

Every trade has two sides: a buyer and a seller. The price is the amount they agree on at that moment. Many buyers and sellers interacting create what we call the market.

03 • WHY PRICES CHANGE

Supply, demand, news and sentiment

Supply & demand

When more people want to buy than sell, prices can rise. When more want to sell than buy, prices can fall.

News & events

News and events can change what market participants are willing to buy or sell.

Market sentiment

When traders feel hopeful they may buy more; when they feel scared they may sell more. Feelings do not guarantee what prices will do.

A simple profit example

$30

05 • HYPOTHETICAL EXAMPLE

Trades can also lose money

BUY → $20
10 shares

RISK IS REAL
−$30

This is a hypothetical example. Prices can fall, and a trader can lose money.

06 • BEGINNER CHECK

What not to believe

“Easy money”

Trading is not easy. It takes practice and skill.

“I can predict the market”

No one can predict prices with certainty. Even experienced participants can be wrong.

“Get rich quick”

Losses are possible and profits are not guaranteed.

“No plan needed”

The lesson emphasizes education, a plan, discipline and rules.

“Fees don't matter”

Fees and taxes can reduce profit and therefore matter.
