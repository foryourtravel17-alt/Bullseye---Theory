Legal & Educational Information

Last updated: September 2026

Educational use

Bullseye Theory provides educational material about trading concepts, markets, chart reading, risk management and trading psychology. The material is not individualized financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Risk

Trading involves risk and losses are possible. Examples, calculations and scenarios are educational and do not guarantee profits or any particular outcome.

Privacy summary

The service may process student name, email, lesson progress, quiz attempts and certificate records to provide account and course features. Authentication and course data are handled through Supabase and the website is delivered through Cloudflare Workers.
