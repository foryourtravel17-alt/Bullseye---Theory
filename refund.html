Last updated: November 26, 2026

Written terms before manual payment

The website has no online checkout or payment method. Any manual enrolment must be individually confirmed by the administrator. Before you make a manual payment, ask for and read the written cancellation/refund terms that apply to that exact product and offer. Do not pay if the terms have not been provided and accepted. The final business-wide refund policy still requires owner and legal review before paid enrolment is scaled.

Questions or a refund request

For a question or request, contact eyetheorybulls@gmail.com with your account email, product, date and relevant transaction reference. Do not email passwords, one-time codes, full card details or wallet recovery phrases. Each request will be reviewed against the written terms supplied for that enrolment and applicable law.
