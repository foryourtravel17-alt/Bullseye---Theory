Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Information we collect

Bullseye Theory may collect account information such as your name and email address, together with course-related information such as lesson progress, quiz attempts and certificate records.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure student accounts, provide course access, track learning progress, administer quizzes and issue certificates.

Authentication and service providers

Authentication and course data are handled through Supabase. The production website is hosted through Cloudflare Workers. Render is used only for isolated preview environments, not the production site. These providers process information as needed to provide the website and student-account features.

Your choices

You can request help with your account or personal information through the support contact published by Bullseye Theory. Account deletion or other data requests may be subject to records that must be retained for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

Contact

For privacy, account, or course-support requests, contact eyetheorybulls@gmail.com. Please do not email passwords, payment-card details, or authentication codes.
