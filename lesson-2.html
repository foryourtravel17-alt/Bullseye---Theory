LESSON 02 • BEGINNER TRADING COURSE

How Financial Markets Work

Financial markets, buyers and sellers, stocks, forex, crypto, supply and demand, and why prices change.

LEARNING GOALS
  • Understand financial markets.
  • Identify stocks, forex and crypto.
  • Explain supply and demand.
  • Understand why prices change without certainty.
01 • MARKET FOUNDATION

What is a financial market?

A financial market is a physical or digital place where people come together to buy and sell financial assets. Its core purpose is bringing buyers and sellers together so trades can happen efficiently and fairly.

Physical

Traditional exchange floors where traders can meet in person.

Digital

Online platforms and apps where financial activity takes place.

Core purpose

Connect buyers and sellers so transactions can occur.

02 • TWO SIDES

Buyers and sellers

Every trade has two sides. Without both parties, no transaction can occur.

BUYERSPeople who want to own an asset or gain market exposure.
VS
SELLERSPeople who want to give up an asset for cash, lock in profits, cut losses, or convert holdings.
03 • CORE CONCEPTS

Three major market types

Stock Market

Buy and sell shares of companies. A share represents a small piece of that business.

Forex Market

Trade one currency for another, such as USD for EUR. The source describes forex as the largest market by volume.

Crypto Market

Buy and sell digital currencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum. The source describes it as newer, highly volatile and around-the-clock.

04 • PRICE ENGINE

Supply and demand

Supply is how much of an asset is available. Demand is how many people want to buy it. The lesson's model: high demand + low supply tends to push prices up; low demand + high supply tends to push prices down.

SUPPLY

Available amount

How much is available to buy at a given time.

DEMAND

Buyer interest

How many people want to buy the asset.

RESULT

Price pressure

The balance can influence price movement.

05 • WHY PRICES CHANGE

News, information and crowd behavior

News & events

Headlines can shift sentiment and buying/selling behavior.

Company earnings

Reports can reveal information and trigger rapid price moves.

Crowd behavior

More buying can push prices up; more selling can push prices down.

Important: price movements cannot be predicted with certainty. This is an educational visual, not a live market.

06 • HYPOTHETICAL EXAMPLE

TechCorp supply & demand example

$50 → $65

1. TechCorp announces a new product customers love.

2. More people rush to buy the stock, increasing demand.

3. In this made-up example, price climbs from $50 to $65.

catalyst → sentiment shift → demand change → price can move

The course material stresses that this is only a mental model. Real markets are more complex and can move unexpectedly even when news seems positive.

07 • RECAP

What to remember

Markets connect people

Buyers and sellers trade financial assets.

Supply & demand

The balance is a core driver of price changes.

Three markets

Stocks, forex and crypto.
