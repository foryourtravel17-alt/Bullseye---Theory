01 / CONDITIONAL OUTLOOK

Scenario Lab

Educational examples · no price targets

BULLISH CONDITION

Continuation after strength

Study case: price holds above a previously observed swing area and participation expands. This is a condition to observe, not a prediction.

WHAT TO OBSERVE
Higher-high / higher-low structure and whether a breakout holds on a retest.
INVALIDATION IDEA
Price falls back into the prior range or the higher-low structure fails.
LEARNING QUESTION
Was the move confirmed, or did you react to one candle?
RANGE / BASE CASE

Two-sided consolidation

Study case: price rotates inside a range without sustained acceptance beyond either boundary. Avoid forcing a directional story without evidence.

WHAT TO OBSERVE
Repeated reactions near range edges and weaker follow-through in the middle.
INVALIDATION IDEA
A confirmed expansion and sustained close outside the range.
LEARNING QUESTION
Is there a clear setup, or is patience the better lesson?
BEARISH CONDITION

Failed support / lower high

Study case: price loses a previously observed support zone and a recovery forms a lower high. The pattern can fail; risk control matters.

WHAT TO OBSERVE
Breakdown, follow-through, retest behaviour and broader timeframe context.
INVALIDATION IDEA
Price reclaims the broken area and restores prior structure.
LEARNING QUESTION
Where would the original thesis be demonstrably wrong?
