BULLSEYE PRINCIPLES

Evidence first.
Risk always.

01

Context before conclusions

Read trend, location, timeframe and surrounding price action together.

02

Risk before reward

Define invalidation, possible loss and position size before acting.

03

Process over impulse

Use written rules, pause when emotions rise and review decisions.

04

Practice before pressure

Use exercises and journaling to build a repeatable learning process.
