THE BULLSEYE METHOD

Study.
Practice.
Review.

Bullseye Theory is designed around a repeatable learning loop. Understand the concept, study the chart, apply the idea in practice, then review the decision and the risk. The goal is a process you can understand and test — not a promise of outcomes.

01 / STUDY

Understand the context

Build the foundations before trying to interpret a live market. Map high-probability market structure levels.

02 / PRACTICE

Apply on charts

Use examples and structured exercises to turn concepts into rapid recognition on real price action.

03 / REVIEW

Refine the process

Review decisions, invalidation and risk so mistakes become quantifiable data to strengthen discipline.
